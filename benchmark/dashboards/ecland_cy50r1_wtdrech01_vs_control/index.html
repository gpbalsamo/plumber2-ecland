Water Scheme Comparison

Water table

The control has no active water-table scheme (LEGWRECHARGE=.FALSE.) — the water table is not a prognostic variable, so there is nothing to pair it against site by site. This version adds a genuine, restart-checkpointed prognostic water-table depth, recharged from diagnosed drainage and throttled by RWTDRECHARGE=0.1, with three water-balance bugs found and fixed along the way (double-counted recharge, an unclamped Fan et al. initial condition, and a multi-layer snow diagnostic reading only its top layer). Network-wide water-balance closure across all 42 sites is now within 4.4 mm/yr of exact everywhere, down from residuals as large as several thousand mm/yr before the fixes.
